How do I schedule a tutoring session?

You can book a session directly through our website by visiting the "Book a Session" page. Choose your preferred tutor, select a time that works for you, and we'll confirm your booking within 24 hours. Sessions can be conducted online or in-person depending on your location.

03

What are your pricing and package options?

We offer flexible pricing with pay-per-session, monthly subscriptions, and custom packages based on your needs. Discounts are available for package deals and long-term commitments. Contact us for personalized pricing that fits your budget.

04

Can I change my tutor if I'm not satisfied?

Absolutely! If you feel that your tutor isn't the right fit, you can request a change at any time. We'll work with you to match you with a tutor whose teaching style better aligns with your learning needs.

05

Do you offer group tutoring sessions?

Yes! We offer small group sessions at discounted rates. Group sessions are great for peer learning and motivation. We typically keep groups to 3-5 students to ensure personalized attention while fostering collaborative learning.
